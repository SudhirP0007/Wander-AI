Help & FAQ.

Common questions about using WanderAI. Can't find an answer? Contact us below.

How do I plan a trip?

Go to Plan Trip and either fill in the Travel Details form or describe your trip in the Chatbot Assistant tab. Our AI will create a day-by-day plan based on your inputs.

Can I change a single day in my itinerary?

Yes. Open your itinerary and click the Regenerate button at the top of any day card. The other days stay exactly as they are.

Where do the prices come from?

Flight and hotel prices are pulled from Amadeus (mocked in this build until API keys are configured). Activity costs are estimates.

Where does the weather information come from?

Daily weather forecasts come from OpenWeatherMap, refreshed when your itinerary is generated or regenerated (mocked without an API key).

Is my data safe?

Yes. Authentication and password storage are handled by Supabase Auth (salted hashing). All traffic is sent over HTTPS. You can request deletion of your account at any time from the Account page.

Can I export my trip as a PDF?

Yes. There's an Export to PDF button on the Itinerary sidebar.

How accurate is the AI-generated itinerary?

WanderAI uses Google Gemini to generate suggestions and cross-references data from Maps, Weather and Amadeus. We recommend you verify opening hours and prices before booking.

Still need help?

Our team usually replies within 2 business days.
